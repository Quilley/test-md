Read and follow [grilling](../grilling/SKILL.md). When defining domain terms or recording architectural decisions, also read [domain-modeling](../domain-modeling/SKILL.md) and apply the project's record conventions.

Capture consequential decisions as they settle, each with its plain name and reason, so the record reads without the conversation. Link the supporting source or reproducible check, preserve material uncertainty, and distinguish the user's chosen direction from claims still awaiting evidence. Keep proposed changes visibly provisional until the relevant choice is made.
