The café has spare machine capacity. A queue still grows. Follow the work to see why.

Read continuously or choose guided stepsIllustrative model

01 · arrival

An order enters the system.

Six orders arrive each minute in our simplified model. The queue contains unfinished commitments to customers, wherever those commitments are stored.

Assume a steady arrival rate. We are not modeling bursts or individual customer waiting times.

02 · preparation

The machine has room.

The machine can make eight drinks a minute. With six arrivals, it is not the limiting step. Increasing its capacity would not change how many drinks the next step can hand over.

03 · completion

The handoff sets the limit.

Only four drinks can reach customers each minute. Six orders enter and four leave, so unfinished work grows by two orders per minute.

The condition: unfinished work grows when arrivals exceed the path's capacity. The location of that work depends on how the café coordinates its steps.

Change the incoming work.

This deterministic model only shows growth pressure on unfinished work. It does not predict average wait or variation. At exact capacity, random bursts could still cause waiting.
