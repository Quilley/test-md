Make the relationship visible.

A small set of useful visual forms, each shaped by the question it answers.

Two changes, different consequences.

Six orders arrive each minute. Preparation can handle eight, while handoff can complete four. The question is which proposed change raises completed output.

Compare capacities on one scale.

These bars share a zero baseline and a scale of twelve orders per minute. They describe the current constructed case.

Show what accumulates.

Starting with no unfinished orders, a constant gap of two orders per minute produces eight unfinished orders after four minutes. This is arithmetic from fixed inputs, not an observed wait.

Intervals can carry meaning.

In this illustrative test schedule, a baseline check is on day one, a pilot review on day seven and a follow-up on day fourteen. Their spacing represents elapsed days.
